# Raffica Write 4.5b: Bookmarks, Captions, Cross-references and Tables of Contents Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A writer can add bookmarks, captions, cross-references, a table of contents and lists of figures and tables in Write, they are always current (order-based results after an editing burst, page-based results when the layout settles, everything before a save), and Word opens every file Write saves without repair and shows the same results.

**Architecture:** A `FieldRefresh` service in `Raffica.Write.Editing` recomputes field results in the model behind an `IFieldHost` that the app implements over the layout; a refresh is a derived change: it does not raise the revision, raises `DocumentEdits.DerivedVersion`, is attributed to the snapshot tracker through `AttributeDerived`, reaches the layout through `ApplyEdit`, and when it changes the number of paragraphs of a table of contents it is recorded as a derived record carried by the newest history step. Commands, cards and a References tab build on that.

**Tech Stack:** .NET 10 Native AOT, Avalonia 12, Open XML SDK 3.x (package layer only), xunit v3 on Microsoft Testing Platform.

**Spec:** `Docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-raffica-write-4-5b-fields-captions-and-tables-of-contents-design.md` (approved 2026-11-26, with "Rules settled after the builder check"). Subproject spec: `Docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-raffica-write-design.md` (goal 8, section 6). Branch: `feature/m45b-fields-captions-toc`. Read the spec's goals, the table of supported fields, "The central design" and the settled rules before any task; this plan cites them by number.

## Global Constraints

- .NET 10 Native AOT, English source, German localization in the "Sie" form; every new command has its own Lucide icon, a reason in every disabled state (`WriteCommandRules`), strings in both `.resx` files (LF), a line in `WriteMacMenu`, no key and no macOS key; no warning suppressed; `dotnet format Raffica.slnx --verify-no-changes` clean; cards go through `CardFooter` and are listed in `CardFooterTests.Entries` **and** the expected list of `CloseGuardTests.CardsAreBuiltByCardFooter`; `WriteCommandCatalog.Find` skips every new id.
- **Supported fields only (spec table):** `SEQ` (`\* <format>`, `\n`, `\c`, `\r <n>`, `\s <n>`), `STYLEREF 1 \s`, `REF` (`\h`, `\n`, `\r`, `\* MERGEFORMAT`, `\* CHARFORMAT`), `PAGEREF \h`, `NOTEREF` (`\h`, `\f`), `TOC` (`\o "a-b"`, `\u`, `\h`, `\z`, `\n [a-b]`, `\c "label"`). Anything else (another name, an unknown token, a missing bookmark) keeps its stored result byte for byte and is marked "not updated"; a refresh never rewrites an instruction. Names and switches are case-insensitive, arguments quoted or not, any order.
- **A refresh is not an edit:** no `DocumentEdits.Raise`, no dirty mark, no recovery copy of its own, no undo step of its own; it raises `DerivedVersion`. Consumers that read text, runs or paragraph identity key on `DocumentEdits.Stamp` (the pair); the dirty flag, `EditHistory`, undo, the save stamp and the recovery scheduler stay on `Revision`.
- **Timing:** order-based results 250 ms after the last edit (never in a key handler); page-based results when `DocumentLayout.IsComplete`, at most 3 passes (then the last result stays and the case is counted); `SettleAsync` before Save; no refresh while a save, an IME composition or a measured run holds the session, for a prefix document, a Faulted session or a layout that is not current; a failure is counted and retried and shows nothing.
- **History:** content-only refreshes are never recorded and run again after every edit, undo and redo; a structural refresh (the paragraph count of a TOC or list changes) is a derived record on the newest step of any kind (undo reverts it first, redo replays it last, the step stops coalescing); empty history or a refused step: nothing is attached; a pending redo: it waits. A structural refresh after an undo or redo that should have restored the state is a bug that the oracle asserts never happens.
- **Editing rules (spec settled rule 2 and 3):** `SEQ`, `REF`, `PAGEREF`, `NOTEREF`, `STYLEREF` results stay atomic as today; a `TOC` is protected as a whole (typing, Enter, paste, a partial cut or delete, a split inside it are refused with a reason; a range covering the whole field deletes it in one step); a paste flattens `TOC`, `REF`, `PAGEREF` and `NOTEREF` and keeps complete `SEQ` and `STYLEREF` fields; `_Toc` and `_Ref` bookmarks and user bookmarks are never pasted.
- **Bookmarks:** names start with a letter, then letters, digits and underscores, at most 40, unique; `_`-prefixed names are Write's (hidden, never listed); a bookmark whose whole range lies inside a deleted range is removed with its end marker, a partly deleted one moves (`EditRules.CollectAnchors`, today's rule).
- **Results:** caption label word by document language, then UI language ("Abbildung", "Tabelle", "Gleichung"), plain editable text; a chapter number is `STYLEREF 1 \s`, a separator and `SEQ <label> \s 1` and needs numbered headings (else refused with a reason); a TOC with no entries shows "No table of contents entries found." in the document's language (German "Es wurden keine Einträge für das Inhaltsverzeichnis gefunden."); entry text: line breaks to spaces, hidden text and tracked deletions left out; entries are `w:hyperlink` to `_Toc` bookmarks in `TOC1`–`TOC9` and `TableofFigures`, with tab and `PAGEREF`; no `w:dirty`, no `w:updateFields`.
- Scope of refresh: body, table cells, block and inline content controls; fields in notes, headers, footers, comments and tracked changes keep their stored result (counted for the bar). A prefix document refuses every command; untitled tabs and recovered copies accept them. During a save every command and key is queued and replayed (4.3a save queue).
- **Incremental equals full** (`LayoutOracle.AssertEqualToFull`, exact) after every command, refresh, undo and redo; every new raise or derived site gets a case in `SnapshotCacheOracleTests` and in `FieldSteps`.
- Start cost: nothing of fields, bookmarks, the References tab or its cards exists before first use; the first settle of a document runs after the layout is complete, never before the first sharp page (`StartupCreatesNoFieldWork`); `WriteRibbon.HomeFullWidthAtLeast` unchanged (`RibbonStartupTests`); `--measure-startup` compared with the commit before 4.5b. Typing at most 16 ms with a 100-entry TOC in the document.
- Out of scope: index, bibliography, table of authorities, `\t`, `\w`, `\p`, `\x`, other `STYLEREF` forms, source formatting for `REF`, a manual update command, fields in notes, headers, footers and comments, hyperlinks and find and replace (4.5c), the owner gates of earlier milestones.

## Review Focus

1. A Word file with a stale TOC, a `TOC \t`, a `REF \w` and a `REF` to a deleted bookmark: open does not dirty it, supported fields refresh on screen, unknown ones stay byte-identical and marked, one bar says how many, Save as writes the refreshed model and a clean Save stays disabled (Tasks 5, 6, 10; `AStaleWordTocIsRefreshedWithoutDirtyingAndWithNothingAttached`, `UnsupportedFieldsKeepTheirBytes`, `ACleanDocumentStaysCleanAndSaveStaysDisabledAfterARefresh`).
2. Undo and redo after a structural refresh with every step kind as the newest step, with an empty history, a refused step, a typing step in progress, and a pending redo: block indices and bytes equal, no corruption (Tasks 2, 10; `TheHistoryMatrix`, `UndoAfterAStructuralRefreshKeepsEveryOlderStepsIndices`).
3. Hostile bookmark names and deletes: 41 characters, a leading digit or underscore, a duplicate with different case, a name from a pasted fragment, deleting the whole range, half of it, and undo (Task 4; `NamesFollowWordsRule`, `DeletingTheWholeRangeRemovesBothMarkersAndUndoRestores`).
4. A caret, selection or IME composition in or beside a refreshed paragraph, a save starting during a refresh, a window closing mid-settle, an edit during the three passes: no stale `TextPosition`, no lost keystroke, the refresh runs later (Tasks 6, 11; `AnEditDuringThePassesStartsOver`, `ClosingTheWindowMidSettleCancelsWithoutAnException`).
5. Headings that are empty, hidden, tracked-deleted, inside a table or content control, without a numbering label, a document with no headings, a TOC whose page numbers change its own length: entries are right or absent, the empty TOC says so, the passes end at three (Tasks 10, 11).
